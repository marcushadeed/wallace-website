**Prelude**

**Preparation for Worship** — In silence, we turn our attention and affections toward our Heavenly Father as we prepare to worship Him.

*A God worthy of our worship calls us to gather and hear the good news of the gospel as he invites us to respond in love, honor, and praise for who he is and what he has done.*

> **People: The children of mankind take refuge in the shadow of your wings.**

> **Leader:** For with you is the fountain of life;
> **People: in your light do we see light.**

❖ **Prayer of Invocation** — We pray celebrating God's promise to bless us with his presence as we worship him.

### III. God Cleanses Us in His Son Jesus

**Confession of Sin**
*Not one of us has lived up to God's standards. This is not because God's standards are wrong, but rather because we're wrong. We've been wrong in how we think, how we act, and what we say. We approach God to acknowledge before him all that is broken and sinful in our lives.*

> Heavenly Father,
> You gave us life and created us in your image, and you have commanded us to honor that image.
> But we confess that we have not always preserved and promoted the life of our neighbor as we ought. We examine ourselves today for all deeds which unjustly take away life. If we have not committed murder, have we acted violently, or incited violence with provoking words? Have we acted in ways that harm the health of ourselves or of our neighbors? Have we nursed in our hearts anger, hatred, envy, or revenge, the seeds of murder?
> Forgive us, we pray, since Jesus laid down his life that we might be forgiven. Help us by your Spirit's power to be more fully ruled by your command to love our neighbor, that our words and deeds might be a fountain of life.
> In Jesus' name, Amen.

**Silent Confession**

**Assurance of Pardoning Grace — John 15:13**
*Here are the words that each of us longs to hear. God tells us that all of our shame, guilt, and sin is removed. Christ has borne our sin, and we now have his righteousness through our faith in him.*
> Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friends.

❖ **Hymn — *O Father, You Are Sovereign*** — Hymn #75 *(children's hymn of the month)*

**Welcome**

**Spotlight** — Robert & Amanda Buntin, Missionaries to Papua New Guinea

**Morning Prayer**
*God invites his people to be a part of his working in the world as they pray to him. We intercede on behalf of each other and our community, presenting our requests to God, and looking forward with expectant hearts to how he will answer in his time and in his way.*

**Giving Our Gifts to the Lord**
*All we are and all we have comes from God. We acknowledge God's goodness in his provision for us by returning to him a portion of what he has given to us.*

**Musical Offering** — Please give glory to God in silent worship during and following the offertory. Thank you for not applauding.
*Children age 4 through 2nd grade may dismiss to the worship hour class upstairs.*

### V. God Communes with Us at His Table

**The Sacrament of the Lord's Supper**
*God invites his people to come, taste, and touch the gospel. The bread and the wine represent the body and blood of Jesus, who died on the cross for sinners. At this table, God feeds his people to strengthen their faith, remind them of their hope, and spur them on to love and good works.*

- Gluten-free wafers are in the center of each communion bread tray.
- If, for reasons of conscience or health, you do not wish to use wine, grape juice is provided in the purple cups in the center of the communion tray.
- Please hold the elements (the bread and the cup) so we may eat and drink together.

**Communion**

**Prayer of Thanksgiving**

**The Encouragement**
> **Pastor:** Peace to you from the God of Abraham.
> **People: Peace to you in the name of I AM.**
> **Pastor:** Consecrate your hearts.
> **People: For the Lord our God is holy.**
> **Pastor:** Let us give thanks to the Lord,
> **People: for he has triumphed gloriously.**

**Words of Institution**

**The Confession**
> **Pastor:** Let us proclaim the mystery of faith:
> **All: Christ has died. Christ is risen. Christ will come again.**
> **Pastor:** Christ has redeemed us from sin and slavery
> **People: That we might be a kingdom of priests and a holy nation.**
> **Pastor:** The gifts of God for the people of God.

*(Servers come forward)*

**Statements for Communion**
- "The body of Christ for you."
- "The blood of Christ for you."
- "You have new life in Christ."
- "You have full forgiveness in Christ."

**Silent Prayers for Those Not Communing**

*For those searching for truth:*
> Lord Jesus, you claim to be the way, the truth and the life. If what you claim is true, please guide me, teach me, and open my eyes to the reality of who you are. Give me the ability to follow you no matter what it might cost me. Give me an understanding of you that is coherent, convincing, and leads to the life that you promise. Amen.

*For those who desire to profess faith in Christ:*
> Lord Jesus, I admit that I am weaker and more sinful than I ever believed, but through you, I am more loved and accepted than I ever dared hope. I thank you for paying my debt, bearing my punishment on the cross and offering forgiveness and new life. Knowing that you have been raised from the dead and are now representing me at the Father's right hand in heaven, I turn from my sins and receive you as my savior. Amen.

*For covenant children who have not yet made a profession of faith:*
> Lord Jesus, thank you for giving me the opportunity to grow up in a Christian family where I am surrounded by the good news of the gospel. Help me to grow in my understanding of who you are and what you have done for me on the cross. Help me learn what it means for me to turn from my sins and trust in you alone for my salvation. Teach me what it means to follow you as one of your disciples. Amen.

*Encouraging struggling and doubting believers to come to the Table:*
> Lord Jesus, give me the ability to see your table as an invitation to grace upon grace; a place to run to in the midst of struggle and temptation. Help me to see that spiritual health and restoration begins with your means of grace and not the other way around. Where I have sinned against others, lead me into reconciliation as you have reconciled us by your blood. Where my conscience is burdened, let me not be my own session but bring my burdens to those you have given to your church for oversight and care. In all matters, help me to see your table as a means to laying aside the sin that clings so closely to me, and run with perseverance the race that you have set before me, looking only to you, the author and perfecter of my faith. Amen.

### VI. God Commissions Us with His Blessing, to Be a Blessing to the World

❖ **Parting Praise — *Thy Mercy, My God***
*Text: John Stocker · Music: Sandra McCracken · ©2001 Same Old Dress Music (ASCAP)*

> Thy mercy, my God, is the theme of my song,
> The joy of my heart, and the boast of my tongue;
> Thy free grace alone, from the first to the last,
> Hath won my affections, and bound my soul fast.
>
> Without Thy sweet mercy I could not live here;
> Sin would reduce me to utter despair;
> But, through Thy free goodness, my spirits revive,
> And He that first made me still keeps me alive.
>
> Thy mercy is more than a match for my heart,
> Which wonders to feel its own hardness depart;
> Dissolved by Thy goodness, I fall to the ground,
> And weep to the praise of the mercy I've found.
>
> Great Father of mercies, Thy goodness I own,
> And the covenant love of Thy crucified Son;
> All praise to the Spirit, Whose whisper divine
> Seals mercy, and pardon, and righteousness mine.
>
> All praise to the Spirit, Whose whisper divine
> Seals mercy, and pardon, and righteousness mine.

❖ **Benediction**
*Just as God had the first word in our worship, he also gets the last word as he sends us out to our homes, work, family, and relationships with his blessing. God blesses us as his children, to live as sons and daughters of the great King, and as brothers and sisters of Christ, and therefore, of one another.*
> The Lord bless you and keep you; the Lord make his face shine on you and be gracious to you; the Lord turn his face toward you and give you peace.

**Postlude**
